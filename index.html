StreetBoy: Beat Rush

            
Rhythm game? Nah. This is beat action.

          

        


        

          

            
StreetBoy is a beat-driven rhythm action game with stylish urban visuals and energetic music. Move through colorful city stages, follow the rhythm, and time your actions to the beat.

            
Tap, jump, and hit — every move connects with the music. Defeat rhythm monsters, keep your combos alive, and clear each stage with perfect timing.
